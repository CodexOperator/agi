**App Purpose: chain-engine**

The chain-engine makes the capillary DAG navigable and visual for LLM agents.

- **Query**: agents ask "what's the longest chain?" and get a ranked list
- **Attract**: agents pick where to work based on attractiveness scores
- **Visualize**: agents see the DAG as Mermaid flowcharts
- **Join/Fork/Hop**: agents extend chains, fork mid-chain, or start fresh per config

Chain-engine = the navigation layer on top of graph-core, turning raw node/edge graphs into actionable research paths.
