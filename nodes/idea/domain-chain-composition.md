How chains from different domains compose, cross-fertilize, and aggregate toward broader outcomes. Currently all 7 idea domains (graph-core, chain-engine, embeddings, renderers, environment-indexers, schema-registry, autoresearch-tree-skill) are isolated capillary threads — their experiment→verdict→MVP→outcome→bigger_outcome chains never connect. Chain-composition enables:
- Cross-domain hypothesis spawning (verdict from domain A spawns hypothesis in domain B)
- Outcome aggregation (multiple domain outcomes → shared bigger_outcome)
- Chain grafting (attaching a standalone chain to a longer chain mid-way)
- The capillary DAG as a true distributed hypergraph rather than 7 parallel threads
