title: "Domain: experiment-automation"
---

Experiment runner infrastructure: taking hypotheses, executing them as experiments, recording verdicts, and emitting MVPs and outcomes. This domain closes the gap between "we have ideas" and "we have working code with documented results." Without this domain, the capillary DAG stays a disconnected idea graveyard.
