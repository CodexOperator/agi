**Big Idea**: Extend shallow 2-hop chains (idea→hypothesis→task) into full trajectories by adding experiment → verdict → MVP → outcome nodes.

The current graph has 88 pending tasks but ZERO experiments or verdicts. All chains are dead-end at depth 2. This domain explores the pattern for completing chains: how to create experiments that generate verdicts, and how verdicts spawn MVPs that produce outcomes.

**Motivation**:
- Longest chain currently = 2 hops (idea → hypothesis → task)
- Chain completion unlocks: proved/disproved status, MVP scripts, outcome documentation
- Without experiments/verdicts, the capillary DAG cannot show what works vs what doesn't

**Key Questions**:
- Where to attach experiments (under which hypothesis)?
- What triggers verdict creation?
- How do MVPs emerge from verdicts?
- What does "outcome" mean in practice?
