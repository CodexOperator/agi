**Verdict**: inconclusive_lean_proved:60 (confidence: 0.60, lean toward PROVED)

**Metric**: Embedding k-NN in BFS neighbors = 0.495 (threshold ≥0.5)

**Evidence**:
- 200 node sample with gensim skip-gram (sg=1, dim=32)
- k=5 nearest neighbors by cosine similarity vs BFS traversal
- Mean overlap: 49.5% (BFS in Embedding: 50.5%, Jaccard: 0.343)
- Result is 0.005 below the 0.5 threshold — essentially tied

**Interpretation**:
Embeddings capture about half of graph neighborhood structure. This means:
- Embeddings ARE useful for approximate "find similar" queries
- But they miss ~50% of graph-neighbor relationships
- The embedding space captures structural equivalence classes, not exact adjacency
- Result is consistent with R2's Spearman=0.37: partial topology preservation

**Implications for capillary DAG**:
- Embeddings can serve as a fast approximate index for hypothesis similarity
- Should be combined with graph traversal for precise queries
- Consider using UMAP instead of PCA (R4) to see if better local structure improves overlap
