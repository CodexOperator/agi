## Experiment: Embedding k-NN vs BFS Graph Neighbors (R3)

**Script**: `exp-a00-324837df-vector-embedding-isomorphism-r3.py`
**Run**: iter-031 agent a00-324837df
**Date**: 2026-05-01

### Method
1. Train gensim skip-gram (same params as R2: sg=1, dim=32, walks=5×40, seed=42)
2. For 200 sampled nodes: find k=5 nearest by cosine similarity in embedding space
3. For same nodes: find k=5 nearest by BFS graph traversal
4. Compute overlap metrics

### Results
- **Embedding k-NN in BFS neighbors: 0.495** (threshold was ≥0.5)
- **BFS in Embedding k-NN: 0.505**
- **Jaccard overlap: 0.343**
- **Sample size: 200 nodes**

### Verdict
**inconclusive_lean_proved:60** — Embeddings capture roughly half of graph neighborhood structure. Just below 0.5 threshold (by 0.005). Genuinely on the edge: embeddings are useful for approximate queries but not perfect graph proxies.
