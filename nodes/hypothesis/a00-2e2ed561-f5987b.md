# hypothesis:a00-2e2ed561-f5987b
## Hypothesis: embeddings/R5 — Skip-Gram vs CBOW Neighborhood Quality

**Testable claim:** Skip-gram (sg=1) produces higher neighborhood fidelity than CBOW (sg=0) on the capillary DAG graph, where fidelity is measured by Spearman correlation with BFS distances and k-NN overlap with BFS neighbors.

**Would prove it:** Skip-gram achieves ≥10% higher k-NN overlap with BFS neighbors than CBOW at k=5.

**Would disprove it:** CBOW matches or exceeds skip-gram on both Spearman and k-NN overlap metrics.

**Background:** R4 (UMAP vs PCA) proved UMAP wins for 2D projection. R3 (embedding isomorphism) measured neighborhood quality for skip-gram alone. This R5 directly compares the two Word2Vec training modes so the renderer/chain-engine can pick the right one for neighborhood-based features.
